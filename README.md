# FuturePath SA

Build FuturePath SA, a South African education and career-discovery platform for learners. Include:
1. Role-based shell and responsive layout (sidebar navigation with mobile drawer) featuring:
   - Dashboard: personalized greeting, progress metrics (Interest Discovery, Career Exploration, Future Planning), quick actions, high-priority tasks, upcoming events and deadlines.
   - Discover: interactive Interest Discovery questionnaire, skills assessment, and interest profile radar/score breakdown (without prescriptive answers).
   - Careers: Career Explorer with search, filters, details on SA career pathways, school subjects, qualifications, and saved careers toggle.
   - My Future Map: interactive visual pathway connecting Interests -> Skills -> Career Areas -> Subjects -> Qualifications -> Institutions -> Application Planning.
   - Education Explorer: South African demo data for Universities, TVET Colleges, Qualifications, Learnerships, Apprenticeships, and Bursaries (NSFAS, Funza Lushaka, etc.).
   - AI Tools:
     * AI Research Assistant: topic/article input producing summaries, key terms, study notes, and revision questions.
     * FuturePath AI Chatbot: interactive chat with study/career prompts, quick questions, message history, and clear tone.
     * AI Task Planner: input natural goals (e.g. "Study Maths, finish science project"), generating structured daily/weekly schedules with breaks, priorities, and editable/completable tasks.
     * Responsible AI disclaimer displayed consistently across all AI components.
   - My Planner: Today, This Week, Calendar view, and Completed tasks with full edit/delete/status controls.
   - Community: School Events (Career Days, University visits), Announcements, and Achievements showcase.
   - Profile & Settings: Learner profile, role switch previews (Learner, Parent, Teacher, School Admin).
2. Modern, friendly, polished design system tailored for South African primary/high school youth, with realistic data, persistent storage, responsive design, and smooth interactions.
